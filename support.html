Subscriptions

Manage or cancel Premium in your store account: on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Android, Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new phone, open Noesis → Premium → Restore purchases.

A new phone

In Noesis, open Profile → Account and backup → Copy my code on the old phone, then on the new phone tap "Restore from code" (also offered on the welcome screen).

Deleting your data

Settings → Privacy → Delete all data.
