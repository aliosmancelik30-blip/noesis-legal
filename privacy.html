Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short

Noesis does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your training results, scores, streaks, settings and the answers you give during onboarding (age range and goal) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage analytics, and we do not share anything with third parties. The app makes no network requests of its own, apart from opening the web pages you choose (such as this one) and talking to the App Store or Google Play when you buy or restore a subscription.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether a subscription is active.

Moving your progress

If you copy a progress code, it is created on your device and contains only your Noesis progress. Where you paste or keep it is up to you; we never receive it.

Deleting your data

Settings → Privacy → Delete all data erases everything immediately. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Children

Noesis is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
